Privacy Policy

Fancy Text Generator & Fonts — by Techi4u
Last updated: September 13, 2026

Overview

Fancy Text Generator & Fonts ("the App") is an offline text-styling utility. The App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send the text you type to any server. Everything you type is converted to a fancy Unicode style directly on your device.

Data We Store Locally

The App stores the following data only on your device (never uploaded anywhere):

  • Your favorited text styles
  • Your recent typing history (used to quickly reuse past text)
  • Your theme preference (light/dark mode)

This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage. We have no access to it.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free access to the App. AdMob may collect and process certain data to serve and measure ads, which can include:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, language, general region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and ad performance metrics)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly. Google may use this data to personalize ads, unless you have opted out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad personalization settings here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings — to manage ad personalization

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require sign-up or login
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contacts
  • We do not upload or store the text you type on any server
  • We do not sell your data

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.
