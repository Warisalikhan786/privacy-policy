Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Qiblah Finder is developed by Techi4u. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information while using our application.

Location Data

The app uses your device's location only to calculate the Qiblah direction and prayer times. This calculation happens on your device, and your precise location is not stored on our servers or shared with us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and share certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID
  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash data

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time from your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send prayer reminders and app updates. This uses a device token to deliver notifications. You can turn off notifications at any time from your device's settings.

Crash Reports and Analytics

We may collect crash logs and diagnostic information to find and fix problems and improve the app. This data does not personally identify you.

In-App Purchases

Premium features are purchased through Google Play. All payments are handled by Google, and we do not receive or store your payment details.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
