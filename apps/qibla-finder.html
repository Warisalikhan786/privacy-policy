Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Qiblah Finder ("the app") is developed by Techi4u. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Location: used on your device to calculate the Qiblah direction and prayer times.
  • Device information: such as device model, operating system and app version.
  • Diagnostic information: such as crash logs and app performance data.
  • App interactions: such as app launches, taps and ad views, collected by third-party services described below.
  • Device identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and notification token.

How We Use Information

  • Show the Qiblah direction and prayer times for your location.
  • Send prayer reminders, Islamic reminders and app updates.
  • Fix crashes and improve app performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent fraud and keep the app secure.

Location Data

The app uses your device's location only to calculate the Qiblah direction and prayer times. This calculation happens on your device, and your precise location is not stored on our servers or sent to us. You can disable location access at any time from your device settings, but the Qiblah and prayer time features may not work correctly without it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and share certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID
  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance data

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time from your device settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services:

Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send prayer reminders, Islamic reminders and app updates. This uses a device token to deliver notifications to your phone. You can turn off notifications at any time from your device settings.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve the experience, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. This information is used to improve the app and is not used to personally identify you.

In-App Purchases

Premium features are purchased through Google Play. All payments are processed by Google, and we do not receive or store your payment details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data is processed and shared with third-party service providers, such as Google AdMob, Google Play Services, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to support advertising, notifications, analytics, crash reporting and core app functionality.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Data Security

All data sent by the app and its third-party services is encrypted in transit. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your precise location is not stored by us. Diagnostic, analytics and advertising data handled by third-party services is kept according to those providers' own retention policies. You can stop future collection of certain data by disabling permissions, turning off notifications, resetting your Advertising ID, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Your Choices

  • Allow or deny Location and Notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection by the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
