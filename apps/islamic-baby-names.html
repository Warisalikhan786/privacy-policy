1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Islamic Baby Names & Meaning (package name com.techi4u.islamicbabynames), published by Techi4u (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the app on your Android device.

By installing and using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect or store any personal information on our own servers. In particular, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Your contacts, messages, photos, files or microphone/camera data
  • Your precise (GPS) location
  • Payment or financial information

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The list of baby names and their meanings is built into the app. Features such as favorites, name comparison and your search or filter choices are stored locally on your device. This data is never uploaded to us. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app, this local data is removed.

5. Your Choices & Controls

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: go to your Android device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads and choose to reset your advertising ID or delete it.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: in the same Ads settings you can opt out of ads personalization.
  • Consent messages: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app may show a consent message so you can manage your ad choices.
  • Go offline: because the app works offline, you can use it without an internet connection, in which case ads and related data transfers do not occur.

6. Permissions

The app may request normal permissions required by the ad service, such as internet access and network state, so that ads can be loaded. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, storage or precise location.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. The only sharing that occurs is the automatic data exchange with Google through AdMob described in Section 4.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, we have no personal data of yours to delete. Data stored locally on your device (such as favorites) can be removed at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google’s policies; you can control it using the options in Section 5.

9. Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience, mainly parents and families looking for baby names. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve the matter.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and keep your information safe. Since the app does not collect personal data on our servers and stores your favorites only on your device, the risk to your privacy is minimal. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
