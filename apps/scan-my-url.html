Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Scan My URL ("the app") is developed by Techi4u. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details. The following information may be collected or processed:

  • Links you scan: the URLs you type, paste or share into the app for checking.
  • Device identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address by our advertising partner. The app itself does not access your device location.
  • App interactions: such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic information: such as app performance data, collected by our advertising partner.

Link Scanning

When you scan a link, the URL is sent to Google's threat detection services to check whether it is known to be unsafe, such as phishing, malware or unwanted software. The app also analyzes the link on your device using its own detection rules.

If you use AI Mode, the link may also be sent to an AI service to help identify suspicious patterns.

Please do not scan links that contain private information, such as password reset links or links with personal details in them.

Important: No link checking service is perfect. A link marked as safe may still be harmful, and a safe link may sometimes be flagged. Always be careful when opening links from unknown sources.

Scan History

Your scan history and app preferences are stored only on your device. We do not store them on our servers. You can delete your history from inside the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and share certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID
  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance data

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time from your device settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data is shared with third-party service providers only to run the app: scanned links are shared with link safety services to check them, and advertising and diagnostic data is shared with Google to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Web Risk / Google Safe Browsing (link safety checks)
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

These services have their own privacy policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The app only requests permissions needed for its features, such as internet access to scan links and receiving links shared from other apps. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or files.

Data Security

All data sent by the app and its third-party services is encrypted in transit. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Scan history and preferences stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data handled by third-party services is kept according to those providers' own retention policies. You can stop future collection by resetting your Advertising ID, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Your Choices

  • Delete your scan history from inside the app at any time.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection by the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
