Hisab Privacy Policy

Offline expense tracker and udhaar book by Techi4u

Effective: 4 October 2026 App: Hisab Developer: Techi4u

In short

  • Hisab works offline. There is no sign up, no login and no account.
  • Your expenses, income, budgets, udhaar, receipt photos and settings are saved only on your phone. Techi4u never receives them.
  • Backups and exports are files that you save where you choose. Nothing is uploaded by the app.
  • The app uses the internet only to show ads (Google AdMob) and to check Google Play for a new version.
  • Google AdMob may collect device data such as the advertising ID to show and measure ads. Details are below.

1.Who we are

Hisab (package name com.techi4u.hisab.offline.expense.tracker) is made by Techi4u. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Techi4u, and "the app" means Hisab. If you have any question about your privacy, write to warisalikhan29@gmail.com.

2.Data you enter stays on your phone

Everything you add in Hisab is stored in a private database inside the app on your device:

  • Expenses, income, transfers, notes, tags and categories
  • Accounts and wallets (for example Cash, Bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa) and their balances
  • Budgets, recurring entries, bill reminders and savings goals
  • Udhaar records: names and optional phone numbers of people you lend to or borrow from
  • Receipt photos you take or pick, copied into the app's private folder
  • Your settings, such as currency and theme

We do not run any server for this data and we never collect, see, sell or share it. Names and phone numbers in the udhaar book are typed by you, stay on your phone, and are never used to contact anyone.

3.Backups and exports

When you create a backup, or export entries as a CSV or PDF file, the app builds the file on your phone and opens your phone's own "Save" screen. You choose where the file goes, for example the Downloads folder or a USB drive. The app does not upload these files anywhere. If you later copy or share a file yourself (for example by WhatsApp, email or a cloud drive), that service's own privacy policy applies. Keep your backup files safe, because they contain all of your entries.

When you restore a backup, the app reads only the file you pick and replaces the data on this phone with it.

4.Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Hisab shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect and send to Google information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location can be guessed
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash reports and performance data of the ads SDK

AdMob does not receive the financial data you enter in Hisab. Google may use this information to show personalised ads, based on your interests, or non-personalised ads. How Google uses it is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice from the app settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ads personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can learn more or opt out at adssettings.google.com.
  • If you buy an ad-free version of Hisab (if offered), ads are no longer shown.

5.Update check

When the phone is online, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version of Hisab is available, and may show an "update available" message. This request only asks for the latest version of the app; it does not include any of your entries. Google Play may see your IP address as part of the request, as with any internet connection.

6.In-app purchases

If Hisab offers a paid upgrade, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. We only learn from Google Play whether the purchase was successful, so the app can unlock the paid features.

7.App lock and fingerprint

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is never stored as plain text. Only a protected (salted and hashed) form of it is kept in secure storage on your phone. Fingerprint or face unlock is checked by Android itself. The app only learns whether the check passed and never receives your fingerprint or face data.

9.Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete single entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Backup files you saved yourself are not deleted with the app; delete them from your files if you no longer need them. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot recover it for you, so please keep a backup.

For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's controls listed in section 4.

10.Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read it. Anyone who can unlock your phone may be able to open the app, so we suggest using a phone lock and, when available, Hisab's app lock. Keep backup files in a safe place.

11.Children

Hisab is a personal finance app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ads in the app, contact us and we will help you reach Google to remove it.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example when a new feature is added. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes may also be shown inside the app.
